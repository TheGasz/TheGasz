## About Me

- Information Engineering undergraduate at **Universitas Gadjah Mada (UGM)**.
- Focused on **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, and Data Science**.
- Passionate about turning multi-source datasets into reliable insights through **EDA, geospatial modeling (`OpenStreetMap`, `Google Earth Engine`, `HydroRIVERS`), and statistical inference**.
- Experienced in **end-to-end Deep Learning & ML pipelines**, including **Hybrid RAG (`IndoBERT`, `FAISS`, `Cross-Encoder`)**, **`SigLIP` + `PEFT/LoRA`**, **`UnetPlusPlus` / `SegFormer` / `DeepLabV3+`**, **`ConvNeXt`**, and gradient boosting ensembles (**`LightGBM`, `XGBoost`, `CatBoost`**).
- Skilled in **Python** and **C++ (OOP)**.
